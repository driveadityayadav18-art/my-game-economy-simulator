## 🎙️ Word-for-Word Voiceover Script

### 📍 [0:00 - 0:25] The Hook & Problem
> *"Hey everyone! Welcome to the **AI-Driven Game Economy Simulator**. In most modern video games and virtual worlds, game economies are either static or suffer from hyperinflation because NPC merchants don't actually react to market dynamics. We built an autonomous multi-agent simulation where AI agents with distinct personalities buy, sell, and speculate in a continuous real-time market—and human players can jump right in to trade, craft, quest, and manipulate the economy."*

### 📍 [0:25 - 0:50] Autonomous AI & 2D Market Plaza
*(👉 **Action**: Click **▶ START SIMULATION**)*
> *"Right now, the simulation is ticking at 4-second intervals. Down here in our Neo-Brutalist 2D Animated Market Plaza, we have **Cora the Farmer** harvesting potions, **Garrick the Greedy** hoarding gems in his gold vault, and **Boran the Adventurer** stocking up on swords. Every tick, our continuous price discovery algorithm updates commodity prices live on our Chart.js telemetry feed, and agents scream their thoughts through floating comic speech bubbles!"*

### 📍 [0:50 - 1:15] Playable Merchant Desk
*(👉 **Action**: Under Player Trading Floor, click `+1` on Raw Gem and click **BUY (GEMS)**)*
> *"As a human merchant, I have full player agency. Here on my Spot Trading Floor, I can buy commodities directly from the order book. Watch as I buy Raw Gems—my gold is deducted, transaction taxes are calculated dynamically, and my purchase instantly increases the clearing price of gems across the entire market."*

### 📍 [1:15 - 1:40] Blacksmith Forge & Expeditions
*(👉 **Action**: Under Blacksmith Forge, click **🔨 FORGE BLADE**, then under Expeditions click **🗡️ EMBARK: DUNGEON RAID**)*
> *"Items in our world aren't just numbers—they have real utility. I can take that Raw Gem and combine it with an Iron Sword in the Blacksmith Forge to craft an **Enchanted Blade**! Or I can take my gear and embark on a high-stakes **Dungeon Raid**—let's go! Boom! +50G loot payout and golden coin particles fountain across the plaza!"*

### 📍 [1:40 - 2:05] Shadow Syndicate & Macroeconomic Shocks
*(👉 **Action**: Under Shadow Syndicate Ops, click **🥷 LAUNCH BANDIT RAID**, then under Game Master Shocks click **🐉 DRAGON ATTACK**)*
> *"If you want to play dirty, the **Shadow Syndicate** lets you launch bandit raids on rivals or fix prices with Garrick. Let's send bandits to rob Cora's gem reserves—she screams for help as her inventory drops! And as Game Master, we can test economic resilience: triggering a **Dragon Attack** wipes out potion supplies, sends potion prices skyrocketing, and floods our market radar with threat alerts!"*

### 📍 [2:05 - 2:20] Conclusion & Technical Rigor
*(👉 **Action**: Scroll to footer / test summary or show terminal)*
> *"Under the hood, everything is built on FastAPI, WebSockets, and Pydantic with **316 automated tests passing at 100% coverage**. It runs out of the box with zero required API keys and can connect to OpenAI, Anthropic, or Gemini for real LLM reasoning. Thank you for watching!"*
